Evidence & confidence rulesDashboard memisahkan dugaan dari konfirmasi. Predict Result 1–4 hanya diperlakukan sebagai prediksi/indikasi, bukan signaling ground truth. Suara putus-putus ditandai bila ada packet loss, jitter, average packet interval atau event counter yang abnormal. Konfirmasi call drop/reconnect memerlukan signaling/session event; konfirmasi silent audio memerlukan RTP dua arah yang tervalidasi atau audio evidence.

Mobility / RF threshold guidePing-pong: same UE returns A → B → A within ≤5 minutes = suspected ping-pong; confirmation requires explicit successful HO events and stable UE identity. RF screening: serving RSRP < -110 dBm, RSRQ < -16 dB, or SINR < 0 dB = poor-RF indicator (screening thresholds, not universal pass/fail limits). Dragging candidate: serving RSRP < -110 dBm while a measured neighbor is ≥3 dB stronger, plus delayed/failed HO evidence. Without neighbor RSRP and HO outcome, classify as insufficient data / mobility risk only. HO failure: any explicit failure/reject/timeout is an event flag; compare against attempt count to calculate failure rate. Cause 21 / RLF: radio connection loss indicator, not proof of dragging or ping-pong by itself. Drive-test/GNSS speed and timestamped serving/neighbor RF are required for definitive dragging diagnosis.
